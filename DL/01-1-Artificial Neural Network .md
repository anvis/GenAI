
Artificial Neural Network (ANN)
- The **foundation** of all neural networks, consisting of input, hidden, and output layers.
- Used for general machine learning tasks, like classification and regression.


- The most basic form of neural networks, inspired by the way neurons work in the human brain.
- Consists of **layers of interconnected neurons**, including input, hidden, and output layers.
- Suitable for **general machine learning problems**, including classification and regression tasks.
- Does **not** have specialized mechanisms for handling spatial (images) or sequential (time-series) data.
