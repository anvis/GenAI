
**Feature Engineering** : Creating Better Inputs

**Feature Selection** : Choosing the Best Inputs

---
Feature Engineering:

The process of transforming raw data into meaningful features that improve model performance. The goal is to Make the data more informative and predictive.
Think of it as crafting new ingredients for your recipe.

Examples:
- From a date column, extract day, month, or weekday.
- Combine “height” and “weight” into a new feature: BMI.
- Encode categorical variables (e.g., one-hot encoding for “color”). 


Feature Selection:

The process of selecting the most relevant features from your dataset. The goal is to Reduce noise, avoid overfitting, and improve model efficiency.
Think of it as picking the best ingredients from your pantry.

Examples:
- Remove features with low variance.
- Use correlation or mutual information to drop redundant features.
- Apply algorithms like Recursive Feature Elimination (RFE) or Lasso.

---

How They Work Together
- Feature Engineering: You create new features like “BMI” or “day of week.”
- Feature Selection: You evaluate which of those features actually help the model and drop the rest.
