
The COSTAR framework is a structured approach to prompt engineering that helps you design clear, effective prompts for large language models. 
It’s essentially a checklist to make sure your prompt covers all the right elements.

---

C – Context::    Provide background or situation.

O – Objective::    State the goal or task

S – Style::     Define tone, format, or persona

T – Tone::     Emotional or communicative flavor

A – Audience::    Specify who the output is for

R – Response::    Indicate the expected format

---

Prompt without COSTAR (basic):
Explain vector databases.

Prompt with COSTAR (structured):

Context: You are preparing a technical presentation for a team of software engineers.  
Objective: Explain vector databases clearly.  
Style: Use layered architecture visuals and structured breakdowns.  
Tone: Professional and educational.  
Audience: Engineers familiar with SQL but new to AI concepts.  
Response: Provide a concise explanation with a diagram and bullet points.
