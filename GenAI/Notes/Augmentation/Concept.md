Normal AI only knows what it learned during his training. If you ask normal AI a question like what were the trending songs on Tik Tok last month, the answer you receive is probably useless. But if you add rag to normal AI, things are different. 

Rag stands for retrieval augmented generation. When you ask a question from rag, it searches through all the resources it has access to like articles, databases, even your own content to find relevant information. Then it combines that fresh relevant information with a built-in knowledge AI has. Then provides you with an answer that is more accurate and upto-ate. 

You've actually seen rag in action. When you ask Chat GPT to use a search feature and answer your question about current situation or trending topics, that's right. In that case, Chat GPT is retrieving fresh information from the internet and combines it with its builtin data and provides you with an up-to-date answer. That's why Chat GPT can tell you about things that happened after his training. 

Now, let's talk about CAG context augmented generation. CAG focuses on adding contextual information directly to the conversation. You've actually experienced CAG with Chat GPT conversations memory. When you tell Chat GPT that you are a student studying biology at the beginning of a chat and then later ask about a studying technique without mentioning biology again, it still recommends you biology specific methods because it remembers that you are a biology student. 

Many systems use rag and CAG together to provide the best results. They retrieve external knowledge and also maintain conversation context. Next time you see an AI giving you surprisingly specific and personalized response, you'll know rag and Cag are working together.
