Time-series data operations in Python are essential for analyzing trends, forecasting, and understanding temporal patterns. Here's a structured breakdown of key operations, along with excellent video tutorials to guide you:

---

### 🕰️ 1. Basics of Time Series
Start by understanding what time series data is and how it's structured.

- Use `pd.to_datetime()` to convert date columns.
- Set `DatetimeIndex` for time-aware operations.

📺 [Pandas Time Series Analysis Part 1: DatetimeIndex and ...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r0s4slGHwzE) explains how to use `DatetimeIndex` and why it's powerful for time-based slicing and plotting.

📺 [Introduction to Time Series - Time Series from Scratch in ...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rnNLr5d0xHs) introduces time series concepts and Python tools for analysis.

---

### 📊 2. Time Series Manipulation
Perform rolling averages, resampling, and filtering.

- `df.resample('M').mean()` for monthly aggregation.
- `df.rolling(window=3).mean()` for smoothing.

📺 [Time Series Data Basics with Pandas Part 1: Rolling Mean ...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OwnaUVt6VVE) shows how to smooth data, remove outliers, and apply rolling statistics.

---

### 📈 3. Forecasting Techniques
Use models like ARIMA, SARIMA, and Exponential Smoothing.

- ARIMA: Autoregressive Integrated Moving Average.
- SARIMA: Seasonal ARIMA for periodic data.

📺 [Time Series Analysis in Python Tutorial - V1](https://m.youtube.com/watch?v=axjgEgBgIY0) walks through ARIMA, Auto ARIMA, and prediction improvements.

📺 [Time Series Forecasting in Python – Tutorial for Beginners](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fxx_E0ojKrc) covers trends, baseline models, ARMA, and cross-validation.

📺 [Time Series Analysis in Python | Time Series Forecasting ...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e8Yw4alG16Q) explains seasonality, stationarity, and ARMA modeling.

---
